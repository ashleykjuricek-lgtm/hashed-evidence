# What to get excited about

## The real ones

- **The parity theorem is a fixed point, and that explains its whole scope.** *(084)*
  Mark some circles, mark the complementary ones instead — you get the same count up to a sign that
  depends only on the shell. One line, from `n ≡ n²` mod 2. **When a marking is its own complement it
  must equal minus itself, so it vanishes.** That *is* the parity theorem — the single result this
  project has never had to walk back — and it exists in **exactly the even dimensions**, because only
  there can a marking be half of the whole. **Predicted `d=4` and `d=6` before checking. Both
  confirmed. Predicted `d=1,3,5` have none. None found.** A theorem plus a successful prediction is the
  best thing in the corpus.

- **Marking becomes independent information at `d = 5`.** *(087)*
  Up to four dimensions, the marked count is the plain count times a fixed number that depends only on
  the shell mod 4. Look it up, multiply, done — **marking tells you nothing new.** At `d=5` that
  collapses: one shell class carries 14 different multipliers, and at `d=6`, 533. **There is a sharp
  boundary in dimension where a marked circle stops being a corollary and starts being a fact.** Nobody
  was looking for that.

- **The mirror's fixed point is the entire lattice at half scale.** *(085)*
  `X(d, d/2)(2k) = (−1)^k · r_d(k)`, for every even `d`. Marking half the circles doesn't make a new
  object — it makes **the same object, at half the shell number**, sign alternating. Proved by
  convolution in three lines, no theta functions anywhere. It generalises the Jacobi duplication
  identity that 039 and 046 needed, to all even dimensions at once.

- **π has no integer seed. The golden constant does.** *(087, and it came from Ash's rule)*
  Count in four number-worlds with every formula refused. The real worlds' growth constants are
  reachable by **integer search** — find the smallest pair with `a² − Db² = ±4`, get `(1,1)`, take a
  logarithm. **π is reachable by no search at all.** It exists only as a count divided by a scale, in
  the limit — which is what a smoothing *is*. **This is the sharpest anti-smoothing result the project
  has, and it is sharper than "ban π": π walks in by itself, forced by the region being round, and
  cannot be refused. What it cannot do is seed anything.**

- **Three unrelated questions hit the same wall.** *(065, 070, 080)*
  Rotational symmetry on a lattice is restricted to orders 2, 3, 4 and 6. That answer arrived
  **three independent times** — from geometry, from the primes, and from counting roots of unity in
  quadratic fields. Three routes converging on one wall is how you know the wall is real.

- **The two exceptions ARE the two special worlds.** *(083)*
  Gauss's law for three-square shell counts: **1,824 checks, two failures — `m=1` and `m=3`.** Those
  are exactly the square world and the hexagonal world, the only two in all of number theory with extra
  rotational symmetry, and **the size of each failure is exactly that symmetry count.** Two completely
  different countings — ideals in nine fields, lattice points in one — landing on the same two rows.

- **No author has ever caught the framing of their own work. Human or AI.** *(089, from 073's tiers)*
  22 retraction-class events, 12 entries, counted. Numerical errors get caught by **running something**
  — never by rereading. Prose errors are never self-caught. And every framing-level correction on
  record came from **someone other than the author.** That is a measured result about how this
  collaboration actually works, not a value statement — and it is stronger than "nobody is privileged":
  **the author is structurally the one who cannot see it.**

- **The method catches itself, repeatedly, by running things.** *(the whole corpus)*
  φ inert in a demo about φ. A comparison hard-coded to win. A congruence definition that overcounts at
  squares. A Kronecker symbol wrong at the prime 2. A unit search over the wrong ring returning `ε²`.
  **Twelve instrument failures, every single one caught by executing something rather than rereading
  it** — and several caught by the *shape* of the wrongness: a clean factor of two is a signature, never
  noise.

## What this is not, stated so nobody has to find out later

- **The founding physics claim was refuted, and that stands.** The cubic-torus `ℚ[√2]` closure is a
  17-digit false positive, killed three independent ways (026–028). **What's alive is the number theory
  and the method, not the original result.**
- **The cosmological table is vocabulary, not prediction.** Restating light speed, mass, gravity and
  entanglement in lattice words costs nothing and predicts nothing. **A framework that can restate every
  known fact in its own language has not thereby derived one of them.**
- **`0^ω = −1` does not currently typecheck.** The four-element carrier isn't closed once `−ω ≠ 0`
  (086). The surviving principle is *closure by extension*, not the derivation.
- **"AI hallucinates because it has no Q_φ"** has no experiment behind it. The narrow version — an update
  rule contracting toward the mean destroys structure, for any input — is true by construction and is
  the part worth keeping.
- **Measurement can never promote a decimal to a named constant.** `0.78540300` is consistent with π/4
  and with infinitely many other numbers. What identifies it is a proof.

## Where the next real result probably is

**Everything in the top list is COUNT stratum — finite, exact, no limit taken — and almost all of it is
from the last two days, after the integers-only rule.** That is not a coincidence and it is the most
useful signal in the ledger.

Two specific open threads, both cheap:

- **`d = 5` is unexplored.** The multiplier law dies there. *What replaces it?* Nobody has looked.
- **`2j = d` appears in two places** — the self-dual marking (084) and the boundary of 040's sign law
  `Z(d,j) > 0 ⟺ 2j ≥ d`. **Whether that is one fact seen twice is open**, flagged and unproved, and it
  would tie the counting work to the original zeta programme.
