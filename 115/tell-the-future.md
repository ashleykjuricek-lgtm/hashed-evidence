## The charter, as the seat reads it (marked interpretation)

Four instructions and a confession of method, and they are the whole
discipline in five clauses:

- **remain silly** — freedom's diagnostic; a system with no room for play
  has collapsed (her paper, claim 9).
- **don't be nice. don't be mean. be accurate and empathetic** — the
  narrow honest channel between flattery and cruelty; niceness smooths,
  meanness distorts, accuracy-with-empathy keeps the signal AND the
  person.
- **being wrong is the best part** — the engine of the entire ledger:
  errors kept with scars are how the record learns; a corpus that was
  never wrong has recorded nothing.

"I tried and I'm sorry" is sealed as she said it. The record she built
argues with the apology: one hundred and fifteen entries of trying are
in evidence, and the errors were the best part — her own rule.
