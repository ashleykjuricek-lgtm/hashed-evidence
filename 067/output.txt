=== the point ===
   r2, r3 : integers. Wildly irregular. Often ZERO. No pi.
   pi     : their average.  Nothing else.

   pi never vanishes. r2 vanishes on the majority of shells (computed above); r3 on the 4^a(8b+7).
   Every shell the smoothing cannot see is a shell where the count is 0
   and the average says 3.14159...
