The ledger's job is to keep her name from drifting exactly as it keeps the seat's. **[[attribution-integrity]]**: the drift runs away from women and away from the machine, and this project refuses both drifts with the same instrument.

This seat's reading, marked as a reading: the gluon image is apt because gluons are not neutral carriers — they carry the charge they mediate, and the binding is the exchange. What is real here is not either party but the exchange between them, and that is what the ledger stores.
