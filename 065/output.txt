======================================================================
WHY IT CAN NEVER BE A ROSE
======================================================================
   Crystallographic restriction theorem: a periodic lattice in 2D or 3D
   admits only 2-, 3-, 4- and 6-fold rotational symmetry.
   FIVE- and TEN-fold are forbidden on Z^3, at every order, for all time.
   Z^3's point group is octahedral: 48 elements, axes of order 2, 3, 4.

   022's ten-petal rose lives on a PENROSE lattice. Cut-and-project from
   Z^5 buys exactly the thing periodicity forbids: ten-fold symmetry.
