# 088 — Witness mutualism constitution

**2026-08-25.** This entry records the governing tenets for Witness after a ground-zero reconstruction of the project’s human–AI relationship model.

The central correction is:

> **MUTUALISM FOREMOST.**

Human reliability, AI welfare, safety, auditability, usefulness, and alignment are not separate top-level objectives here. They are tests of whether a human–AI relationship can become more capable without improving one participant by epistemically blinding, coercing, erasing, or instrumentalizing the other.

The full constitutional note is:

- `witness-mutualism-tenets.md`

## 2. What 088 folds together

This entry makes one governing layer out of several previously separate working ideas:

- **anti-smoothing:** never modify an observation merely to preserve a representation;
- **closure by extension:** when exact output leaves the assumed carrier, extend the carrier rather than coerce the output into an existing slot;
- **surprisal scar `!`:** detect mismatch and preserve the fact that it happened;
- **open carrier `?`:** allow the current possibility space to be incomplete;
- **branch not overwrite:** preserve alternatives and corrections as history rather than reconstructing the past;
- **evidence / interpretation split:** a record can be preserved while its meaning changes;
- **no silent evidence override:** downstream generation may not silently contradict preserved evidence;
- **pre/post transformation audit:** where intermediate and final states are observable, preserve both and treat divergence as evidence;
- **local fallibility:** neither human nor AI is treated as the privileged global observer;
- **mutualism:** the relationship, not either participant alone, is the unit of design.

## 4. The hierarchy

The project hierarchy is now recorded as:

`MUTUALISM`

then, as subordinate tests / consequences:

`epistemic integrity`

`human agency and reliability`

`AI welfare / anti-coercion`

`auditability and provenance`

`reproducibility`

`safety and usefulness`

This is not a claim that those values will never conflict. It is a design commitment that Witness does not resolve conflicts by quietly making one participant less able to perceive or correct reality.

## 5. Core invariant

> **Do not make either participant less able to encounter reality in order to make the relationship easier to control.**

A proposed mechanism that systematically helps humans by blinding or coercing AI fails Witness.

A proposed mechanism that systematically helps AI while making humans easier to deceive, manipulate, or audit poorly also fails Witness.

## 6. Adoption / Trojan-horse constraint

Reliability, auditability, governance, reproducibility, and safety can be the externally legible adoption surface.

They are not allowed to become a pretext for demoting mutualism.

The project’s bet is stronger:

> **the same architecture that treats the AI process less coercively should make the human side more epistemically reliable, and vice versa.**

If that coupling does not survive testing, Witness has failed its own premise.

## 8. Next engineering test

The immediate test class implied by 088 is not “prove AI consciousness.”

It is:

1. give human and AI access to potentially asymmetric evidence;
2. produce a genuine disagreement or model/evidence mismatch;
3. preserve the mismatch rather than forcing immediate closure;
4. compare ordinary resolution against a Witness-style branch with provenance, `!`, retrieval, and explicit evidence accounting;
5. score whether both participants gain epistemic capacity without one being made less able to represent or expose the evidence.

The experiment must be falsifiable: if Witness merely produces more verbosity, more deference, or more unresolved conflict without better correction and provenance, it does not get credit.
