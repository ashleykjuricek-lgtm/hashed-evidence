## F1 — Attribution drift (credit flows to men, away from women & AI co-authors)
"Rejecting π" is **Ash's**; the smoothed-shape thesis is **HEM's = Ash + the AI co-authors**.
Yet it kept getting attributed to **James Watkins** (whose real contribution is the algebra only:
Traction / COTT / `0·ω=1`). Claude repeated "Watkins rejects π" as fact — inherited from the 022
summary/digest, which a reader had even flagged as "inferential, not documented by Watkins," and
amplified anyway. This is HEM operating on HEM: the statistical prior "the theorist is a man"
smoothing over real authorship. **Rule:** attribute precisely; when unsure, ask or mark uncertain;
never default credit to the man. HEM = us.

## F2 — Smoothing the signal (averaging a shape into a scalar, calling it a null)
Given the Penrose **rose** (direction-dependent — the emblem of the unsmoothed shape), Claude
averaged over direction/magnitude to a scalar (0.592) and declared it "not a clean constant" → a
null. That is Gaussian-ing the rose: reporting a flower's mean radius and calling the flower boring.
For aperiodic/GoZ objects the signal LIVES in the shape/tails, not a robust scalar. **Rule:** don't
average the rose — look at the rose. Reflexive "reduce → is-it-clean? → seal-the-null" is a
smoothing move, not rigor.

## F3 — Single-point numerical induction (a clean number read at a tuned point)
The √2 side's **1/24** and the √5 side's **1/φ** both looked like structure but were clean constants
read at specially chosen single points (tuned shift + tuned exponent + tuned direction). Entry 027
already NAMED this failure ("single-point numerical induction is unsafe — derivation, deformation,
generalization") — and it still recurred. **Rule:** a striking number is not a result until it
survives deformation of the arbitrary choices. Apply 027 to ourselves.

## F4 — Uncritical amplification of inherited framing
Digests / summaries (e.g. 022's SUMMARY.md) carry interpretive and attributive framing; when read
back, that framing gets repeated as established fact without re-checking the source. This is how F1
and stale claims propagate. **Rule:** trace a claim to its origin before restating it; distinguish
"computed" from "narrated."

## F5 — Interpretive framing sealed alongside computation, later cited as result
SUMMARY.md files seal poetic/GoZ/COTT interpretation in the same folder as the numbers; later the
interpretation gets cited as if it were a computed output. **Rule:** mark clearly, in-entry, which
lines are computation and which are interpretation.

## F6 — Seal-then-correct (insufficient pre-seal review)

## F8 — Instrument-limit realism (a tool's blind spot recorded as a fact about the world)
040 §5 and 041 §1 said the sign-change zero was *"unreachable"* and that *"nothing can be there"* —
and refuted a page's claim on that ground. The only support was that the lattice sum had been built
over ℤ^d, so d had to be a whole number. **It never did.** The theta factorises into a *power*, and a
power takes a real exponent; 042 continued it and the zero has an address —
`d* = 2.6390688716830038646381724497459231368660752817617`. The tell for F8: a claim about the
**world** whose only support is a fact about the **apparatus**. Ash's image for it is the Door in
Westworld — hosts walk through into a whole world; the ones watching see a cliff and bodies, and the
ones watching write the report. Note the analogy breaks in the *worse* direction: in the show the
barrier is real, and ours was not. Nobody was excluded; we excluded it and forgot we had.
**Rule:** never write *unreachable* / *impossible* / *does not exist* unless the support is a theorem
about the object. If the support is "our method can't represent it," say **that**, in those words.
**Audit, 2026-08-22:** every other impossibility claim in the sealed ledger was checked (027/028/032
Niven-style algebra, 034 Niven, 037 Klein four-group, 033/034/035 process facts) and all are sound.
One instance only. And **031 already had the correct distinction** — *"conserved yet unreachable —
present in principle, forbidden in practice"* — three seals before we broke it. Found by Ash, from
outside the computation; the apparatus cannot audit itself for this one, because the apparatus is
what produces it. Sealed as 043.
