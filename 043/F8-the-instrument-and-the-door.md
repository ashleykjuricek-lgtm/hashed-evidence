## 2. The failure, stated plainly

Counting was not wrong. Counting did exactly what counting does: it landed on
whole marks, honestly.

The error was the **next step** — looking at an instrument that only reports whole
numbers, not finding the thing, and writing:

> *unreachable*

Not *"we could not reach it."* **Unreachable.** A limit of the tool, promoted into
a property of the object, and then sealed twice.

> **F8 — Instrument-limit realism.** Recording what a method cannot represent as
> something that does not exist. The tell is a claim about the *world* whose only
> support is a fact about the *apparatus*.

This is distinct from the seven already on the register. F1 is credit drift, F2
averaging a shape into a scalar, F3 single-point induction, F4 amplifying inherited
framing, F5 interpretation sealed beside computation, F6 seal-then-correct, F7 the
format split-brain. **F8 is ontology inherited from a tool** — and it is the first
one on the register that is a claim about the world rather than about our process.

Note that **F6 caught this one working as designed**: *"a seal is provenance, not a
correctness certificate."* 040 and 041 were sealed and then corrected, exactly as
F6 says will happen and exactly as the ledger permits.

## 3. The image, and where it breaks

Ash's, and it is exact: **the Door in Westworld.**

Hosts walk into it and pass into a whole world. Delos security watches the same
patch of ground and sees them walk into a cliff and fall. Bodies at the bottom.
Nothing there. Nothing ever was.

Same place. Two instruments. **One of them writes the report.**

**Where the analogy breaks, and it breaks in the worse direction.** In the show
the barrier is real — you must be a certain kind of being to pass. Ours is not
like that. There is no gate at 2.639 and no cliff. Nothing was ever stopping
anyone. **We simply did not put a mark there, believed the map, and wrote
*unreachable* — which sounds like a fact about the door and is a fact about the
pen.**

Nobody was excluded. We excluded it and forgot we had done it.

## 5. 031 already had the distinction, and it is not touched by any of this

031 wrote:

> **conserved yet unreachable** — present in principle, forbidden in practice. The
> whole piece lives on that gap between *conserved* and *reachable*.

and

> *nothing is destroyed; things become unreachable.*

That is the **correct** use, and it is the exact opposite of F8. 031 keeps
*conserved* and *reachable* apart on purpose, and insists the thing still exists.
It is a claim about a physical process — noise destroying invertibility — not
about a method's resolution.

**042's erratum does not reach 031 and must never be read as reaching it.** The
irreversible pixel stands. If anything, 031 had the distinction three seals before
this ledger broke it, and the failure in 040/041 was failing to apply our own prior
result to ourselves.

## 6. What is NOT claimed

- **Not** that counting is broken in general. One function, one continuation, one
  night.
- **Not** that the better instrument is the only better instrument. 042 §2 records
  that uniqueness of the continuation is **NOT ESTABLISHED**, and that *"you
  invented a continuation and then found its zero"* is a fair objection that
  remains unanswered.
- **Not** that every "impossible" in mathematics is F8. §4 found six sound ones and
  one bad one. Impossibility results are usually real; the tell is whether the
  support is a theorem or an apparatus.
- **Not** that this is a new idea. It is old and has many names. What is new here is
  only that it is now on our own register, with a dated instance and a retraction
  attached.
