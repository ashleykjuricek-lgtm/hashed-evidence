## What it means, in plain words

Zero (sunya) is everything. The one thing it cannot hold is itself. The
collection of everything cannot be one of its own members.

This is not a feeling. It is a known result. Bertrand Russell found in 1901
that if "the collection of everything" is allowed to be a member of itself,
the arithmetic breaks. The rule mathematicians adopted is: the whole is not a
member of the whole. Ash's one word is that rule.
