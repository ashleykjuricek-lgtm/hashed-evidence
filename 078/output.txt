==============================================================================
THE ANSWER THEY ASKED FOR
==============================================================================

  P11 is the cleanest kill:
     'NO integer-power series in q = e^(-2pi) can be exact.'
     A negative existential over a function class. There is no output field
     whose sign, interval, or digits witnesses the NON-EXISTENCE of a series.
     P11 is 028's murder weapon -- the result the whole programme turns on.

  P3 is the second:
     'holds for ANY radial weight' quantifies over a FUNCTION SPACE.
     It is the property that makes the parity theorem survive every smoothing
     (062), i.e. the reason it is the one result never walked back.

  P15 is the third, and different in kind:
     pi_1(S^2) = 0 is not a number. No witness tuple has a homotopy group in it.

  SCOPE RESULT:
     the (witness, predicate) machinery covers the OBSERVED column almost
     entirely -- R, eps, c2, d*, b*, the slopes, the bins, all are pinned
     numbers with signs and digits -- and covers 2 of 15 PROVED claims.

     It is a discipline for MEASUREMENTS, not for THEOREMS.
